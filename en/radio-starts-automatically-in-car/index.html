How to make the radio start on its own when you get in the car

Vesko from Drive Radio

Let me start with what used to annoy me. I get in, start the engine, the phone is in my pocket. I take it out, unlock it, look for the radio app, tap "Play". Every day, several times a day. And the phone and the car had already connected over Bluetooth on their own, so the phone knew I was in the car. Only the radio didn't.

That's why I made Drive Radio. Here is how it works, after months of driving with it every day.

What happens when I get in

I start the car. A second or two later the phone connects and the radio starts on its own, with the last station I was listening to. The phone stays in my pocket. I don't unlock it: it works on a locked screen too, I've checked that many times.

And if you lose internet for a moment on the way, in a tunnel or out of coverage, the radio comes back by itself as soon as the signal returns. No need to press "Play".

For me Waze opens as well, and the music keeps playing. When you get out and the connection drops, the radio stops and the navigation leaves the screen, so the phone can turn its screen off by itself.

I've tried it in different cars, with a regular Bluetooth head unit and with Android Auto, and on different phones.

Setting it up

  1. Pair your phone with the car over Bluetooth, as usual.
  2. In Drive Radio, pick the car's Bluetooth.
  3. Choose the station you want to greet you. There is plenty to choose from: the search goes through Radio Browser, an open worldwide catalog of over 50,000 internet radio stations.

That's it. Next time, the radio starts on its own.

One thing I learned the hard way

Some phones, most often Xiaomi, Huawei and Honor, stop apps in the background to save battery. Then the radio simply doesn't start, and people think it doesn't work.

That's why, the first time you open it, Drive Radio shows you which permissions your phone needs and takes you to them. It takes a minute, but it's the difference between "sometimes it starts" and "it always starts".
