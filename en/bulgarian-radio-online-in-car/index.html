Bulgarian radio stations online in the car

Vesko from Drive Radio

People often ask me whether Drive Radio has "their" stations. Drive Radio has no station list of its own. The search goes through Radio Browser, an open worldwide catalog of internet radio kept up by listeners and the stations themselves. It has plenty of Bulgarian stations, from pop and rock to pop-folk and news, so the answer is almost always yes.

How I find a station

I pick Bulgaria and see the stations ordered by how often people listen to them, the most listened first. I've removed the duplicates and the dead ones, so I don't have to dig. If I know which station I want, I type its name in the search. The ones I like get a star, so they're always at hand.

I listen when I'm abroad too

Because the radio comes over the internet, Bulgarian stations sound just as clean in Germany, Greece or Spain. Many Bulgarians living abroad use it exactly like that, to hear something from home on the road.

I like the song. What is it?

Drive Radio shows the song title and the album art. One tap opens it in Spotify, YouTube Music or whichever app you use, so you can save it for later.

At home and on iPhone too

If you want to listen on your computer or on an iPhone, there's a web player. Your favorite stations are the same there and on your phone.

If your station is missing

You can add it yourself. Open the station's website in Drive Radio's built-in browser, press Play there and the app catches the stream. You can also paste the stream address if you know it.

Or write to me at info.driveradio@gmail.com and we'll add it to Radio Browser. That way everyone else gets it too.

Try it in your car. Listening is free.
