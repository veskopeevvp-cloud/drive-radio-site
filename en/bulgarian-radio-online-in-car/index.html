Bulgarian radio stations online in the car

Vesko from Drive Radio

People often ask me whether Drive Radio has "their" stations. The answer is almost always yes: there are hundreds of Bulgarian stations inside, national, regional and music ones, from pop and rock to pop-folk and news.

How I find a station

I type the station's name in the search and play it. If I don't know what to listen to, I browse by country. The stations I like get a star, so they're always at hand.

I listen when I'm abroad too

Because the radio comes over the internet, Bulgarian stations sound just as clean in Germany, Greece or Spain. Many Bulgarians living abroad use it exactly like that, to hear something from home on the road.

I like the song. What is it?

Drive Radio shows the song title and the album art. One tap opens it in Spotify, YouTube Music or whichever app you use, so you can save it for later.

At home and on iPhone too

If you want to listen on your computer or on an iPhone, there's a web player. Your favorite stations are the same there and on your phone.

If your station is missing

Write to me at info.driveradio@gmail.com. The stations come from a large worldwide catalog of internet radio, and if one is missing we'll add it there. That way everyone else gets it too.
